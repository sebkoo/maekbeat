# Disclaimer

Maekbeat is an educational portfolio project. It is **not a medical device**.

## Not for clinical use

Maekbeat is not cleared, approved, or reviewed by the FDA or any other
regulator. It must never be used for diagnosis, monitoring, or treatment of
any medical condition.

## No seizure-detection claims

Maekbeat does not detect seizures. It is being built to demonstrate a
pipeline of the kind used in seizure-monitoring research: synthetic vitals
over a BLE-style transport, a Node.js relay (shipped — C5–C12a), a caregiver
dashboard (shipped — C10–C13), and an iOS app that reads that relay, forwards
to it, and notifies on its alerts (shipped — C14–C17); the sequence is in
docs/ROADMAP.md.

## Synthetic data only

All vitals in this project are generated by a simulator
(`packages/vitals-sim`, planned — lands at C2). No data from real people,
devices, or patients enters this repository at any point.

## No protected health information

Maekbeat processes no PHI. There are no real patients, no real caregivers,
and no clinical records anywhere in this codebase.
